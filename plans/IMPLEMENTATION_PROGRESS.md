## Shared contracts

- **Intake.** Save the study area as an exact revision and document the intended use, time interval, coverage, units, source rights and quality criteria. Check retained surface, geoid, imagery and vector inputs before acquiring missing members. Bind the area, source decisions and processing limits to a versioned profile. [Geographic intake and commissioning](../docs/implementation/ENVIRONMENT_LIBRARY_OPERATIONS.md) · [Quality and profile evidence](../docs/implementation/GEOGRAPHIC_SOURCE_QUALITY_AND_STORAGE.md#f02-profile-and-geographic-quality)
- **Identity and publication.** A display pins its parent release and manifest digest, exact input members, recipe and toolchain. Published bytes have their own size and SHA-256. Scoped idempotency keys reject a repeated request with changed content. Publication commits under a live lease, and reconciliation handles uploaded objects without a committed reference. [Display API](../api/openapi/environment-display.yaml) · [Worker API](../api/openapi/environment-worker.yaml)
- **Rights and compatibility.** Online reads enforce source grants and applicable original and current rights. Portable issuance carries its own terms. Apply additive schema changes before rolling out the API, update readers before producers, and fence incompatible attempts. Verify the existing releases and a paired restore against the nominated binaries. [Operations and upgrade evidence](../docs/implementation/GEOGRAPHIC_OPERATIONS_AND_UPGRADE.md#f10-contracts-and-compatibility)
