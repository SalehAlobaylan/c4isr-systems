# Supporting sources by exercise decision

Use these plans when the exercise owner can identify a decision that the extra source could change. Each row specifies a first deliverable and a check that can fail. Retain the provider's native time, units and geography. Distinguish a valid zero, no coverage, a provider error and an observation still pending at the exercise cutoff.

Atlas and the offline worker now replay supporting evidence. The delivery record includes the owned case, historical provider checks and remaining source qualification. [Supporting evidence delivery](supporting-sources-delivery.md)

## Which event or observation merits a second look?

| Source | Feature and first delivery | Acceptance case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USGS earthquake catalog ([usgs](normalization.md#usgs)) | Select a historical event for a civil inspection drill. Reuse the event adapter and pin event ID, origin time, revised magnitude and last update. Use field observations for any road or building condition. [USGS catalog](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/) | Replay two editions of one event. The count stays one and the reviewer can see when the revision became known. |
| NASA FIRMS ([firms](normalization.md#firms)) | Offer a list of sites for environmental follow-up after a thermal detection. Retain sensor, acquisition time, footprint or pixel size, confidence where supplied, archive type and missing-data notices. [FIRMS API](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/) | Compare two overpasses, one with unavailable observations. The view distinguishes the data gap from an observed detection and draws only the supported observation extent. |
| GDACS ([gdacs](normalization.md#gdacs)) | Begin a historical disaster-response timeline with a dated bulletin. Preserve event/episode identifiers and revisions, with the issuer's estimate clearly labeled. [GDACS overview](https://www.gdacs.org/About/overview.aspx) | A later severity update changes the later briefing. An inspection verdict must continue to reference its own field evidence. |
| NASA EONET ([eonet](normalization.md#eonet)) | Discover additional imagery or reports for a historical event. Retain event geometry dates and upstream sources, then match the source reference against any retained GDACS bulletin. [EONET v3](https://eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/v3) | Two records pointing to the same bulletin appear as two publications supported by one underlying source. Closing a catalog entry leaves the independently recorded restoration date intact. |
| NOAA-20 orbital pilot ([noaa20](normalization.md#noaa20)) | Help an imagery reviewer explain a possible observation opportunity during a historical episode. Reuse the existing civil pilot, pin the archived orbit epoch, and look for an actual dated scene in the imagery catalog. [CelesTrak data formats](https://celestrak.org/NORAD/documentation/gp-data-formats.php) | A possible pass with no qualifying image leaves imagery availability unknown. A later published scene becomes available at its own release time. |
| ICESat-2 ATL13 inland water levels ([atl13](normalization.md#atl13)) | Show whether an inland-water inspection has a comparable historical measurement. Search one reservoir for two dated along-track measurements. Preserve product version, height reference and quality flags. [NSIDC product](https://nsidc.org/data/atl13) | Compare elevations only after reference and quality checks. Show no measurement between tracks. A street flood-depth question would require local hydraulic and ground observations. |
| Sentinel-5P ([s5p](normalization.md#s5p)) | Give the environmental reviewer a regional before/after atmospheric comparison. Start with one named product such as tropospheric NO₂, common units, a fixed area and product-specific quality filtering. [Copernicus product documentation](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s5p-products) | Report valid observation area and sample counts for each interval. A cloudy interval stays incomplete. Ground-level exposure needs independent surface measurements. |

## Can civilian reports and services recover from an interruption?

| Source | Feature and first delivery | Acceptance case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloudflare Radar ([radar-outages](normalization.md#radar-outages)) | Let an exercise author compare normal report delivery with an authored reporting delay during a documented regional Internet outage. Import a dated annotation, its scope, start/end and update times with permitted read access. Model delay and message backlog separately. [Radar annotations](https://developers.cloudflare.com/api/go/resources/radar/subresources/annotations/) · [Access guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/radar/get-started/first-request/) | Run identical reports through both queues. Show ingestion lag, peak backlog and recovery duration. The annotation alone cannot determine local delay or the status of a particular facility. |
| GPSJAM ([gpsjam](normalization.md#gpsjam)) | Train a reviewer to distinguish a low-confidence daily regional navigation-quality summary from a lack of observations. Check archival access, permissions, daily window and provider definitions first. Use a coarse historical summary or a labeled fixture for the offline exercise. [Provider methodology](https://gpsjam.org/faq) | A missing cell and a degraded-quality cell produce different explanations. The debrief scores whether the reviewer sought another observation and preserved uncertainty about cause. |
| HDX HAPI ([hapi](normalization.md#hapi)) | Give the planner a coverage browser for a named humanitarian indicator. Query by country, admin level, reference period and upstream resource. Show which candidate regions have usable data before enabling a comparison. [HAPI documentation](https://hdx-hapi.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) | A covered area reproduces its source value. Missing Saudi area coverage remains unavailable. A republished indicator links to its original release. |
| UNHCR statistics ([unhcr](normalization.md#unhcr)) | Compare regional scenarios with a dated displaced-population baseline. Select one defined population group and distinguish year-end stocks from flows when importing reference periods. [UNHCR methodology](https://www.unhcr.org/refugee-statistics/methodology) | Compare like definitions and periods and keep country of origin distinct from country of asylum. Avoid adding the count to residential estimates without evidence of disjoint populations. |
| IOM DTM ([dtm](normalization.md#dtm)) | Let a planner compare changes between two comparable assessment rounds, preserving method, geography, survey dates, reporting unit and completeness. Start with one publicly shareable operation. [IOM DTM data access](https://dtm.iom.int/data-and-analysis/dtm-api) | A boundary or method change flags a break in the series. A location omitted from the next round stays unknown. Confirm a suitable local operation before using it in a Saudi exercise. |
| Cable and infrastructure listings ([infrastructure](normalization.md#infrastructure)) | Use a sourced inventory as background for a fictional service-recovery exercise. Add exercise-authored dependency, outage and restoration records with distinct provenance. For ports, use the identifier crosswalk in the coastal plan. [Catalog](https://www.worldmonitor.app/docs/data-sources) · [Port inventory plan](03-regional-response-and-coast.md) | Deleting a fictional dependency changes the exercise queue. A published map line by itself has no effect on service status. Field validation would require dated service interruptions from an authorized operator. |

## Would conditions or their publication time change an inspection?

| Source | Feature and first delivery | Acceptance case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open-Meteo forecasts and archive products ([meteo-runs](normalization.md#meteo-runs) · [meteo-history](normalization.md#meteo-history)) | Compare an inspection scheduling decision with the forecast initially available and the later historical estimate. Select a model, units and point/grid interpretation. Keep initialization, validity and availability. A single-run forecast preserves a forecast horizon, while stitched historical forecasts serve retrospective summaries. [Single runs](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api) · [Historical forecasts](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api) · [Terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) | Two forecasts valid at the same hour remain distinct. A later run stays hidden at the earlier cutoff. Document the service arrangement for the intended use. |
| ERA5 ([era5](normalization.md#era5)) | Give the analyst a retrospective environmental reference when debriefing an observed condition. Extend the retained Saudi pilot to a named interval and variables, preserving native grid, vertical levels and units in the existing field reader. [Existing Saudi slice](../../../docs/implementation/RIYADH_OBSERVATION_PILOT.md) · [ERA5 pressure-level archive](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-pressure-levels) | Reproduce reference values at two grid times and a missing level. Display native and interpolated values distinctly and keep reanalysis outside the information available in a historical dispatch decision. |
| Aviation Weather Center METAR, TAF and international SIGMET ([metar](normalization.md#metar) · [taf](normalization.md#taf) · [sigmet](normalization.md#sigmet)) | Add an airport timeline to a civil disruption exercise. Start with one archived station observation, a forecast and an area advisory that cover the selected exercise where available. Preserve station/area, issue, validity, amendment and cancellation as appropriate. [AWC API](https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/) | Show METAR as observed at the station, TAF for its forecast period and SIGMET for its stated area/time. Test one amendment and an uncovered interval. Qualify each product's archive independently. |
| WMO directory and Saudi NCM CAP ([ncm-cap](normalization.md#ncm-cap)) | Replay delivery of an Arabic or English public notice and let a coordinator acknowledge later changes. Follow the WMO issuer listing, obtain a permitted Saudi sample, then preserve original identifiers, languages, times and linked updates. The earlier assessment could verify the listing only. [WMO Saudi authority](https://alertingauthority.wmo.int/authorities.php?recId=140) | Test an update, a cancellation, a failed fetch and both language versions of one notice. Count the underlying notice once where the publisher establishes equivalence. |
| CAMS dust via Open-Meteo ([cams](normalization.md#cams)) | Add regional dust context to a reviewer deciding whether an image requires another inspection. Record native grid and time, units, aggregation and interpolation. Saudi coverage uses the global product, roughly 45 km for the documented CAMS Global grid. [Air quality product](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api) | An area intersecting one model cell reports one underlying value. Evaluate any claimed relationship with image usability against held-out, independently rated images. |
| Open-Meteo marine ([marine](normalization.md#marine)) | Add sea-state context beside a recorded coastal inspection. Start with one variable, model and dated offshore interval. Document coverage, grid and vertical reference where applicable, then compare with an independent observation if available. [Marine API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/marine-weather-api) | Test a valid offshore cell and a shoreline or land sample. Show unavailable coverage explicitly. Defer harbor-scale claims until local observations support them. |
| RainViewer ([rainviewer](normalization.md#rainviewer)) | Defer a radar playback dependency. The transition notice limits remaining products and describes discontinued nowcasts. For a visual playback need, check Saudi coverage, historical availability and intended-use permission first. [Provider transition notice](https://www.rainviewer.com/api/transition-faq.html) | Admit only a demonstrably covered and permitted visual example. A rendered color tile supplies no calibrated rain-rate input without a separately verified quantitative product. |

For the installed public adapters, respect product scope. FIRMS covers the admitted NOAA-20 VIIRS product, EONET covers flood reports, and the installed GDACS adapters cover earthquake bulletins. An additional category, forecast product or new field needs its own sample, mapping and acceptance case. Keep an unqualified source optional until the scenario requires its measurement. ## Other catalog domains

Historical news or an event database can help an analyst discover a *civil service disruption* for a debrief. The first deliverable would be a source citation, event date, first publication date and independent service or restoration record. Two articles quoting one statement count as one origin. Airport delay statistics can support an aggregate passenger-disruption exercise once a provider supplies dated airport counts, cancellation definitions and scheduled-flight denominators. Compare one day with the provider's published total before computing rates.

Financial feeds, prediction markets, composite risk scores and broad strategic inventories have no defined input or success measure in these exercises. Leave them in the discovery catalog until a separate exercise specifies what a user would do differently with the data.
