# Civilian aerial inspection delivery

Atlas opens **Civilian aerial inspection replay** from a non-withdrawn Environment Library release. Enter the admission and source IDs for `civilian-aerial-review` `v1`, an independently supplied package SHA-256, a UTC cutoff and the reviewed JSON. The service checks the selected release manifest and the source's current internal-analysis rights. The operator can inspect a before-and-after pair, toggle the local quality mask and an available flooded-road suggestion, request a clearer view, record imagery evidence or refer the case for field inspection. Field inspection and the road replay provide the reopening evidence. [Aerial plan](02-aerial-inspection-and-aviation.md) · [Road access plan](01-reports-roads-and-inspections.md)

The review follows each scene's `availableAt`, aligned native grid, preview SHA-256, usable area and minimum review width. An optional flood overlay has its own availability time. The review shows CEMS assessment extent status and source-image lineage, staged handoff cues, historical civil traffic coverage, dated publications and survey-cell coverage. It counts overlapping survey cells once and retains aborted assignments in assignment totals. A handoff submission downloads as JSON for a facilitator to score against a separately held rubric. [Review request and response contract](../../../api/openapi/environment-library.yaml)

## Prepare and score evidence

`scripts/data/prepare_aerial_review.py --help` lists the preparation actions. `earthsearch` reads a retained STAC response, distinguishes partial catalog pages, and pins the requested scene's aligned previews. `--metadata-only` retains a catalog candidate with missing assets. `cems` matches the declared vector layer to a finished product and derives coverage over the requested bounds. `attach-overlay` requires an integer class raster matching the preview CRS, affine transform and pixel dimensions. `sans-index`, `traffic` and `service-notice` prepare dated publications and reviewed historical extracts. `energy-summary` imports aggregate results from the completed-flight receipt. `assemble` joins the fragments to a named area revision and records the underlying source digests. [Preparation commands](../../../scripts/data/prepare_aerial_review.py)

The historical traffic CSV contains `day`, `availableAt`, `civilCount`, `unclassified`, `usableHours` and `oldestAt`. Empty count cells represent unknown coverage. The notice CSV expects an issuer-stable `id`, one `deliveryId` per service, a numeric `revision`, `publisher`, `title`, `publishedAt`, `availableAt`, `effectiveAt`, optional `expiresAt`, and `status`. The replayer combines matching issuer records and checks that duplicated deliveries agree. [Aviation field definitions](normalization.md#opensky) · [Notice field definitions](normalization.md#icao-notam)

`scripts/data/evaluate_aerial_review.py` prepares the `airlab-definition`, executes the `energy` comparison, scores `flood` review submissions using the pinned integer masks and scores downloaded `handoff` actions. The flood evaluator checks disjoint reviewer-assigned collection and visual groups, computes flooded-road IoU, counts false clears with the flooded-case denominator and records abstention and review time. Match the test cases across the unaided, baseline and assisted runs. Keep the answers and the submission receipt in the evaluator's workspace. [Evaluator commands](../../../scripts/data/evaluate_aerial_review.py) · [Known-answer tests](../../../scripts/data/test_evaluate_aerial_review.py)
