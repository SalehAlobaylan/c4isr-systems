[GEBCO 2026 product and use limitations](https://www.gebco.net/data-products/gridded-bathymetry-data/gebco2026-grid)

The sign and source type agreed within those 299 cells, which measures consistency in this sample. Physical accuracy remains unknown. GEBCO combines vertical datums in some shallow areas. Navigation, port and survey decisions, water levels and physical seabed models require separate qualified inputs.
